I’m Tokenimizer, principal developer at [Hidden Tokens](https://hiddentokens.com/). I’m building a way to use AI with real documents while keeping personal details private: Hidden Tokens anonymises Word, Excel, and PDF files on your computer before an AI service reads them. The AI works with codes; the real values can be restored afterward.

I’m starting this journal to share more of the work behind that idea. A product page can explain what a tool does, but there’s a lot around the edges worth documenting too: the decisions, experiments, trade-offs, and setups that shape how it gets built.

Here I’ll write about developing Hidden Tokens, privacy-first AI workflows, the tools I use, and the setups that help me work. I’ll share what I learn as I go, including things I change my mind about. I’ll keep private or customer-sensitive details out of it.

If you’re interested in the product itself, you can find it at [hiddentokens.com](https://hiddentokens.com/). This journal is where I’ll share the thinking and work around it.
